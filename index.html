Why it exists

Every NFT ever minted pretends to be permanent. KEEPALIVE makes care visible. Each token carries a real deadline in block numbers. Anyone — not just the owner — can breathe life into it, and every breath leaves the breather's mark woven into the flame.

The art is not a picture of the game. The art IS the state: breath count raises the flame, sparks multiply, the current keeper's address glows in the smoke. Neglect it, and the SVG itself goes grey.

  • Entropy capsule: each ember's DNA is the hash + basefee + gas price of its own mint block — a portrait of the network at the second it was born.
  • Stewardship, not ownership: strangers' gas becomes collective care. The griefing vector is empty — the worst a griefer can do is keep everything alive.
  • Agent-keeper: an AI agent (Bankr on Base) breathes the collection daily from the lung fund — a machine tending a fire, on-chain.
  • ERC-4906: every breath emits a metadata update, so marketplaces re-pull the new art instantly.
  • Zero dependencies: no IPFS, no metadata server, no oracle. If Base persists, every ember persists.
